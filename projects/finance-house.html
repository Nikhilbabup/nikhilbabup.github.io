Finance House

The backend behind Finance House’s LLC and PJSC businesses, with bilingual content, investor relations and financial reporting.

Smart Hatch TechnologiesBackend & APIsEnglish / Arabic

The problem

Finance House runs two very different businesses. The LLC sells financial products. The PJSC is publicly listed and must publish investor relations material and financial reports. Both need bilingual (English and Arabic) content managed safely from one platform, without the two units stepping on each other.

What I built

  • RESTful APIs on Node.js, TypeScript and Express, backed by PostgreSQL through Sequelize.
  • English and Arabic content across the platform.
  • Secure auth with JWT and bcryptjs, plus Helmet, CORS and rate limiting.
  • XSS protection: rich content is sanitised with sanitize-html, xss and DOMPurify before it is stored or served.
  • Request validation on every endpoint with Joi.
  • File handling: uploads with Formidable, image optimisation with Sharp, zip archives with adm-zip, and Excel import and export with ExcelJS.
  • Structured logging with Winston, and tests with Jest.
