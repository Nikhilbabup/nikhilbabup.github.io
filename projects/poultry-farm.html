PoultryPro

PoultryPro is the Flutter app I built to run my own poultry farm. It covers flock count, mortality, egg production and sales, orders, finances, feed purchases and reports, and it works fully offline.

The problem

I started a poultry farm with 45 chickens and grew it to 200. At that size, tracking orders and counting eggs on paper stopped working. Records were hard to keep up to date, totals were hard to add up, and it was difficult to see whether the farm was making money.

What I built

  • Farm records: a live chicken count that updates as mortality is logged, daily egg production, egg sales and customer orders, income and expenses, and feed purchases.
  • Offline-first storage: every record is saved to a local SQLite database, so the app works in the field with no signal.
  • Cloud backup and sync with Firebase Firestore once a connection is available.
  • Device-to-device sync over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, so records can move between phones without the internet.
  • Reports and analytics: charts of production and finances with fl_chart, PDF reports to print or share, and CSV export for spreadsheets.
  • Backup and restore from local files.
  • Riverpod for compile-safe state management, with swipe-to-act lists and shimmer loading states for a polished feel.

Results

  • Replaced paper records for the whole farm.
  • Supported the farm as it grew from 45 to 200 chickens.
  • Production, orders and finances live in one place instead of scattered notebooks.
