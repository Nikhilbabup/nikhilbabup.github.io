Poultry Farm Manager

A Flutter app I built to run my own poultry farm: flock count, mortality, daily egg production, orders, income, expenses, feed purchases and reports.

Personal projectFlutter mobile app45 → 200 birds

The problem

I started a poultry farm with 45 chickens and grew it to 200. At that size, tracking orders and counting eggs on paper stopped working. Records were hard to keep up to date, totals were hard to add up, and it was difficult to see whether the farm was making money.

What I built

  • Flock tracking: the live chicken count, updated as mortality is logged.
  • Mortality logging, so losses are recorded the day they happen.
  • Daily egg production logs.
  • Order management for egg customers.
  • Income and expense tracking.
  • Feed purchase records.
  • Reports that bring production, sales and costs together in one view.

Results

  • Replaced paper records for the whole farm.
  • Supported the farm as it grew from 45 to 200 chickens.
  • Production, orders and finances live in one place instead of scattered notebooks.
