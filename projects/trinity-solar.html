Trinity Solar Portal

A portal where customers manage their solar energy systems.

Backend & APIsCustomer portal

The problem

Solar customers needed one place to manage their energy systems and get timely notifications about them.

What I built

  • Customer portal APIs built on Adonis.js and Lucid ORM.
  • JWT-based authentication.
  • SMS notifications through Twilio.
