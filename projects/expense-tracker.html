Expense Tracker

A personal finance app I built in Flutter: track spending and income, scan receipts with on-device OCR, and watch savings goals fill up automatically.

Personal projectOn-device OCROffline-first

The problem

Keeping track of personal spending is tedious. Typing in every receipt by hand gets skipped, it's hard to see where the money actually goes, and savings goals end up tracked somewhere else entirely, if at all.

What I built

  • Receipt scanning: on-device OCR with Google ML Kit reads text from a photographed receipt, so entries don’t have to be typed in.
  • Expense and income tracking, kept separate, with categories and notes.
  • Receipt attachments: attach a photo from the camera or gallery and preview it on the transaction.
  • Savings goals whose progress updates automatically from your income and expenses.
  • Dashboard with charts of your financial health (fl_chart).
  • Reminders through scheduled local notifications, and CSV export to share your transactions.
  • Offline-first: all data lives on the device in Hive, a fast NoSQL store.
  • Engineering quality: Riverpod with code generation for state and dependency injection, unit tests with Mocktail, and strict linting (flutter_lints, riverpod_lint).
