Tutoring marketplace

Logos Learning

A marketplace where students hire tutors for specific subjects and book sessions online or in person.

WebandcraftsBackend & APIsMarketplace

The problem

Students needed one place to find tutors for a subject, check that the tutors are vetted, and book sessions around tutor availability, over video or in person. Tutors needed to get paid.

What I built

  • Availability-based session booking.
  • Video sessions through Dyte.
  • Tutor background checks through Checkr.
  • Payments through PayPal and Authorize.net.
  • Real-time updates over Socket.IO.
  • The admin panel APIs for tutor and student management and tutor payouts.
