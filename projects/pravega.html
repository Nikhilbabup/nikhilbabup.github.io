Payment switch middleware

Pravega

Middleware that connects ATMs and point-of-sale systems to payment switches and card management systems.

Applied PaymentsPayments infrastructureATM / POS

The problem

ATMs and POS terminals must reach payment switches and card management systems reliably and quickly. Every transaction is real money, so dropped or slow messages are not acceptable.

What I built

  • Queue-based, high-throughput transaction processing on BullMQ and Redis.
  • Found and fixed a critical packet-splitting issue in transaction message handling.

Results

  • The packet-splitting fix improved overall transaction processing speed.
